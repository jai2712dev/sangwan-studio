# Outreach messages

## 1. First message
Hi! I help small businesses improve their websites and online presence. I can fix mobile display issues, update content, repair buttons/links, or make a simple landing page with your services and contact details.

My introductory price for small fixes starts at ₹499, depending on the work. If you're interested, I can first point out 2–3 improvements I notice on your current site. Would that be useful?

## 2. For a business without a website
Hi! I came across your business and wanted to ask whether you have a website or digital menu/service page. I build simple mobile-friendly pages that show your services, location and WhatsApp contact. I can share a sample and a fixed quote before any work begins.

## 3. Follow-up after 2–3 days
Hi, just following up on my message about website improvements. No pressure—if this isn't something you need right now, that's completely fine. If useful, I can send a short list of possible improvements.

## 4. Quote confirmation
To confirm, I will: [deliverables]. Price: ₹[amount]. Deadline: [date]. Included revisions: [number]. Anything outside this list will be quoted separately. Payment terms: [advance/balance]. Please reply “Agreed” if this matches what you want.

## 5. Ask for a review/referral after successful delivery
Thanks for working with me. If you're happy with the result, could you send me 1–2 lines of feedback I can quote with your permission? If you know another business that needs a website fix, an introduction would also help.
