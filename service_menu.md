# Starter service menu

Keep the first jobs small and easy to deliver.

| Offer | Introductory price guide | Include | Exclude unless quoted |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Small website fix | ₹499–₹799 | One clearly described small fix | Full redesign, paid plugins |
| Mobile responsiveness pass | ₹799–₹1,499 | Fix agreed layout issues on key pages | Rebuilding the whole site |
| Digital menu/service page | ₹799–₹1,499 | One page, provided text/images, contact links | Domain, paid hosting, photography |
| One-page business landing page | ₹1,499–₹3,000 | Responsive sections, contact CTA, basic metadata | Copywriting beyond agreed amount, backend, ongoing maintenance |

These are starting hypotheses, not guaranteed market rates. Start with a written scope and a small job you can finish confidently.

## Before accepting a job
- Ask for the website URL and exact desired change.
- Confirm who owns the site and has permission to request edits.
- Confirm the deliverables, price, deadline, and revisions in writing.
- Do not accept work involving impersonation, phishing, malware, or unauthorized access.
- Use a preview/staging copy when possible; back up files before changing a live site.
- Get approval before publishing changes.
