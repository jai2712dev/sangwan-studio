GOOD TO KNOW / 06

Questions, answered.

A few useful details before we start a conversation.

How much does a website cost?＋

Pricing depends on the number of pages, content, features and condition of any existing website. We’ll discuss your requirements and provide a quote before starting.

How long does a project take?＋

Timelines depend on the scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided. A realistic schedule is agreed before work begins.

Can you fix my existing website?＋

Often, yes. We first review the website and identify whether the issue is a suitable frontend fix or requires backend access or specialist work outside our current scope.

Will my website work on mobile?＋

Responsive behaviour is part of our website design process. We check the layout at mobile, tablet and desktop widths.

Do you provide hosting and domain names?＋

We can guide you through suitable hosting and domain options. Domain and hosting charges, if needed, are separate from the website design quote.
