# Daily operating workflow (3–5 hours)

## Every day
1. **Find 10 relevant prospects (45–60 min).** Look for small businesses with no website, outdated mobile layout, broken links, or no clear contact CTA. Use public business contact channels.
2. **Personalize 10 messages (45–60 min).** Mention one genuine, observable improvement. Do not send identical bulk spam.
3. **Follow up with older leads (20 min).** One polite follow-up after 2–3 days; stop if they decline or do not want further contact.
4. **Build or improve a demo (45–90 min).** Use fictional/sample content or get permission before using a business's branding.
5. **Deliver client work / learn (60–120 min).** Prioritize paid deliverables over endless portfolio polishing.
6. **Update tracker (10 min).** Record status, next action, and amount agreed/received.

## Metrics to track
- Prospects researched
- Personalized messages sent
- Replies
- Calls/meetings
- Quotes sent
- Jobs won
- Cash actually received

## Reality check
A high outreach count does not guarantee sales. Review the message and target segment after 30–50 well-targeted contacts. Don't spend money on ads until a service has sold organically.
